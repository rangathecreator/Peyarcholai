# 07 – Additional Value-Add Features

Prioritised by user value vs. effort. Items 1–4 are the proposed differentiators beyond the core.

| Priority | Feature | Value | Effort | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Name evaluation scorecard | High | Medium | See `03-Name-Evaluation-Engine.md` |
| P1 | "Vote out a name" social poll | High (viral) | Medium | See `04-Social-Poll-Feature.md` |
| P1 | Nakshatra-based initial-syllable suggestions | High in India | Low-Medium | From birth date/time/place; needs ephemeris library |
| P1 | Multi-language UI and native-script search | High | Medium | Hindi + 3–4 languages first |
| P2 | Pronunciation audio (TTS) | Medium-High | Low | Cloud TTS |
| P2 | Shortlist & compare side by side | High | Low | Core UX |
| P2 | Partner (spouse) collaboration: both parents swipe/shortlist, show matches | Medium-High | Low-Medium | Reduces disputes |
| P2 | PDF "naming certificate" / keepsake | Medium | Low | Revenue (premium) |
| P2 | Sibling/family name harmony check | Medium | Low | Part of evaluation |
| P3 | Initials / monogram check (avoid unfortunate initials) | Medium | Low | Easy and delightful |
| P3 | Document-friendliness check (Aadhaar/passport length limits, transliteration consistency) | Medium | Low | Practical differentiator |
| P3 | International usability (name meaning/pronunciation abroad for NRIs) | Medium | Medium | NRI market is valuable |
| P3 | Naming-ceremony (namakaran) planner: muhurat, checklist, invitation cards | Medium | Medium | Partnerships/affiliate revenue |
| P3 | Domain / social handle availability for the name | Low-Medium | Low | Modern parents |
| P3 | Name trends by region and year | Medium | Medium | Needs frequency data |
| P4 | AI assistant: "suggest names like X in Tamil, starting with S, 3 syllables" | High | Medium | Must ground answers in our verified DB |
| P4 | Expert consultation marketplace | Medium | High | Commission revenue |
| P4 | Renaming / business / pet naming | Low-Medium | Low | Audience expansion |
| P4 | Offline mode | Medium | Medium | Useful for low connectivity |

## Guardrails
- Keep family decision control with the parents; avoid features that promote pressure or shaming.
- Show numerology/astrology as cultural guidance with a disclaimer.
- Protect children's and family data (DPDP Act); minimise data collection.
