# NamingGuru Revenue & Monetization Strategy

**Date:** 2026-11-10
**Currency:** INR
**Context:** Web-first MVP (Next.js), 10,600 Tamil names, 14-dimension evaluation.
**Core Constraint:** India-first, Tamil diaspora second. Free tier is the primary acquisition engine.

## 1. Strategic Positioning & Pricing Philosophy

NamingGuru sits at the intersection of **high-intent utility** (finding a name) and **emotional/cultural significance** (child's identity). The monetization strategy must balance:
1.  **Low Friction Acquisition:** Users are often in a "research" phase. Aggressive paywalls kill conversion.
2.  **Trust & Ethics:** The "Honesty Layer" (provenance, no fear-based astrology) is a key differentiator. Monetization must not undermine this trust.
3.  **B2B Potential:** Hospitals and clinics are high-volume, low-transaction-cost channels.

**Pricing Philosophy:**
*   **Free Tier:** Full access to search, filters, basic numerology, and shortlisting. This drives SEO and word-of-mouth.
*   **Premium (One-time & Subscription):** Unlocks deep-dive reports, AI advisor, and "Shareable PDFs" for family consensus.
*   **B2B:** White-label widgets or API access for maternity hospitals and baby product brands.

### Lifetime Value (LTV)
*   **Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) per Month:**

## 7. Legal & Ethical Guardrails

1.  **No Fear-Based Astrology:**
    *   **Prohibited:** "Buy this gemstone to avoid bad luck," "This name will cause illness if not corrected."
    *   **Allowed:** "Numerology suggests this name aligns with your birth number," "Nakshatra analysis indicates these starting syllables are traditional."
    *   **Implementation:** All AI-generated text must pass a "Fear-Filter" check. No "remedy" language.

2.  **Transparency in Sponsored Content:**
    *   All sponsored listings must have a visible "Sponsored" badge.
    *   Algorithmic ranking must not be influenced by sponsorship.

3.  **Data Privacy (DPDP Act):**
    *   No sale of user data to third parties.
    *   Clear opt-in for marketing emails.
    *   Data deletion request honored within 7 days.

4.  **Affiliate Disclosure:**
    *   Clear disclosure on affiliate links: "We may earn a commission if you buy through these links, at no extra cost to you."

5.  **Content Accuracy:**
    *   All name meanings and numerology calculations must be clearly labeled as "Illustrative" or "Based on traditional systems" to avoid liability for "false promises."
