## 1. Classical Grammar & Root Sourcing: Tolkappiyam (தொல்காப்பியம்)

*Tolkappiyam* is the earliest extant work of Tamil literature and grammar. It provides the morphological, phonological, and thematic foundation for authentic Tamil name formation.

### 1.1 Structural Divisions for Name Sourcing
1. **Eluthathikaram (எழுத்ததிகாரம் — Phonology & Orthography)**:
   - Defines pure Tamil initial letters (*Muthal Ezhuthu*).
   - Rules of *Udambadumei* sandhi (உடம்படுமெய் — நூற்பா 141):
     $$\text{இ ஈ ஐ வழி யவ்வும் ஏனை உயிர்வழி வவ்வும் உடம்படுமெய் என்றாகும்}$$
   - Authentic Tamil vowels and consonant-vowel combinations suitable for naming.
2. **Sollathikaram (சொல்லதிகாராம் — Morphology & Semantics)**:
   - *Peyariyal* (பெயரியல்): Noun formation rules, distinguishing personal nouns (*Iyarpeyar*), derived nouns (*Aakkappeyar*), and quality nouns (*Panbuppeyar*).
   - Rules preventing ungrammatical suffixing (e.g., masculine `-ன்` nouns taking secondary personal suffixes).
3. **Porulathikaram (பொருளதிகாரம் — Poetics, Landscapes & Human Emotions)**:
   - **Ainthinai (ஐந்திணை — Five Landscapes)**:
     - **Kurinji (குறிஞ்சி)**: Mountains, union, flowers (*Kurinji*, *Kanthal*), morning sun.
     - **Mullai (முல்லை)**: Forests, pastoral life, patience, jasmine (*Mullai*, *Thondri*).
     - **Marutham (மருதம்)**: Agricultural plains, rivers, lotus (*Aambal*, *Kamalam*), prosperity.
     - **Neithal (நெய்தல்)**: Coastal shore, sea, pearls (*Muthu*), water lily.
     - **Palai (பாலை)**: Arid journey, courage, victory, determination.

## 2. Epic Literature Sourcing: Kamba Ramayanam (கம்பராமாயணம்)

Authored by Mahakavi Kamban in the 12th century, *Kamba Ramayanam* (கம்பராமாயணம் / இராமாவதாரம்) is one of the greatest masterpieces of Tamil epic poetry. It adapts the epic with profound Tamil cultural sensibilities, philosophical depth, and unparalleled poetic lyricism.

### 2.1 Epic Cantos (காண்டங்கள்)
1. **Balakandam (பால காண்டம்)**: Youth, virtue, archery, education, nature descriptions of Sarayu river (*ஆற்றுப் படலம்*) and Kosala country.
2. **Ayodhya Kandam (அயோத்தியா காண்டம்)**: Righteous conduct, brotherly love (*குகப் படலம்*), filial devotion.
3. **Aranya Kandam (ஆரணிய காண்டம்)**: Forest life, Panchavati nature, valor of Jatayu (*சடாயு காண் படலம்*).
4. **Kishkindha Kandam (கிட்கிந்தா காண்டம்)**: Friendship, loyalty of Hanuman and Angada.
5. **Sundara Kandam (சுந்தர காண்டம்)**: Devotion, bravery, eloquence (*சொல்லின் செல்வன்*), hope.
6. **Yuddha Kandam (யுத்த காண்டம்)**: Triumph of Dharma over Adharma, forgiveness, righteous governance.

## 3. Sangam Corpus Expansion (சங்க இலக்கியம்)

பெயர்ச்சோலை (Peyarcholai) actively mines authentic vocabulary and historical poet/chieftain names from the primary Sangam anthologies:

### 3.1 Ettuthokai (எட்டுத்தொகை — The Eight Anthologies)
1. **Akananuru (அகநானூறு)**: 400 poems on love, family, and domestic virtue across the five landscapes.
2. **Purananuru (புறநானூறு)**: 400 heroic poems celebrating kings (Karikalan, Nedunchezhiyan), queens, charity (Pari, Pegan, Ay, Athiyaman), and universal philosophy (*"யாதும் ஊரே யாவரும் கேளிர்"*).
3. **Natrinai (நற்றிணை)**: 400 classical landscape and emotion poems.
4. **Kurunthokai (குறுந்தொகை)**: 401 concise gem poems on devotion, integrity, and rain/nature.
5. **Aingurunuru (ஐங்குறுநூறு)**: 500 short poems specifically categorized by *Ainthinai*.
6. **Kalithokai (கலித்தொகை)**: Musical poems rich in rhythm, joy, and societal ethics.
7. **Paripadal (பரிபாடல்)**: Devotional hymns celebrating Murugan, Thirumal, and the sacred Vaigai river.
8. **Pathitrupathu (பதிற்றுப்பத்து)**: Epic praise of Chera kings, valor, and ethical sovereignty.

### 3.2 Pattuppattu (பத்துப்பாட்டு — The Ten Idylls)
- *Thirumurugatruppadai*, *Porunaratruppadai*, *Sirupanatruppadai*, *Perumpanatruppadai*, *Mullaippattu*, *Maduraikkanchi*, *Nedunalvaadai*, *Kurinjippattu*, *Pattinappalai*, *Malaipadukadam*.

## 5. Ingestion & Quality Control Protocol

Whenever a candidate name is extracted from Tolkappiyam, Kamba Ramayanam, or Sangam literature:
1. **Direct Presence Substring Check (`CIT-01`)**: If labeled `Classical / Sangam Tamil` or `Medieval Epic`, the exact Tamil word must literally appear in the cited text/verse.
2. **Lexicon Cross-Reference (`CIT-02`)**: Sourcing script generates direct URL to the DSAL Madras Lexicon query.
3. **Compound Separation (`CLA-02` / `FMT-01`)**: If the name is newly combined from epic roots (e.g., `அன்பு + அரசி`), it must be labeled `Modern Tamil` (`🧪 Modern Synthesis`) and must never claim to be an ancient verse itself.
4. **Confidence Gating**: Confidence score must be $\ge 0.80$ to author citations in the catalog.
